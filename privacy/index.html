Privacy Policy — SpyGunner

Last updated: 20 November 2026

SpyGunner is a single-player spy shooter game that runs entirely on your device.

  • The game does not collect any personal data and sends no information off the device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no network connection.
  • There are no real-money purchases. All in-game rewards are virtual.
  • Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device only and are removed when the app is deleted.

Contact: see the support page.
